# Derived-data codebook

## `validation_all_candidates_curated.csv`
Curated rows for all 77 algorithmically prioritized candidate topics.

## `validated_topics_strict.csv`
The strict expert-included (`Yes`) topic subset used for the principal biological interpretation.

## `axis_topic_membership.csv`
Mapping from strict expert-included topics to the four non-mutually-exclusive biological axes derived from expert primary and secondary labels.

## `core_axis_prevalence_annual.csv`
Annual 2011–2025 topic-axis counts, total eligible GBM denominator, raw prevalence percentage, and centered 3-year rolling prevalence.

## `temporal_axis_trend_stats.csv`
Spearman trend statistics for the four biological axes with Benjamini–Hochberg-adjusted q values.

## `sentinel_topic_prevalence_annual.csv`
Annual prevalence for the four sentinel topics: 79, 16, 11, and 63.

## `sentinel_topic_trend_stats.csv`
Spearman trend statistics for the four sentinel topics, with a separate Benjamini–Hochberg correction family.

## `validation_summary.csv` / `validation_summary.json`
Aggregate validation counts and expert score summaries.
